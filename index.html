01 / the mission

We optimized for absolutely nothing.

Except making your feed increasingly yours. idkvideos learns from what you actually watch, skip, like and dislike — then gets progressively more suspicious about your interests.

You wanted a Shorts viewer.
We accidentally built a progression system.
You wanted one video.
We gave you a lifetime achievement.
02 / features

Small features.
Unreasonable consequences.

01

Personal feed

Your watch behaviour shapes discovery instead of becoming one giant anonymous “everyone likes this” blob.

02

Completion XP

Finish a Short. Get one point. Skip it? Nice try. Your title must be earned.

03

Doomscroll mode

Auto-scroll can keep the feed moving while your remaining free will quietly leaves the building.

04

Regional brain

Discovery can adapt to your region and language context without turning your feed into generic global soup.

05

Ridiculous levels

Passive Fiend. Doomscroller. And eventually something so absurd it should not be physically possible.

06

Official player

Videos are streamed through YouTube's official embedded player. idkvideos does not host or download them.
